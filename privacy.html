Terms of Service · Privacy Policy BiteWise Privacy Policy Last updated: October 16, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how BiteWise ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information when you use the BiteWise app (the "App"). We aim to collect only what we need to help you reach your diet and fitness goals. 1. Information We Collect Account information: name, email address and password (stored hashed). Profile and health information you provide: age, sex, height, weight, goals, dietary preferences, allergies, meals and foods logged, water intake and activity. Data from connected services (optional): with your permission, steps, workouts or weight from Apple Health, Google Health Connect or similar services. Photos (optional): meal photos or barcodes you choose to scan. Device and usage data: device type, operating system, app version, crash logs and feature usage. Purchase information: subscription status from the app store. We do not receive your full payment card details. 2. How We Use Your Information To provide core features such as calorie and macro tracking, meal plans and progress charts. To personalize recommendations and goals. To send reminders and notifications you have turned on. To maintain security, fix bugs and improve the App. To respond to support requests and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your health data for advertising. 3. Sharing We share information only with: service providers who host, store or process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations (for example cloud hosting, analytics and crash reporting); app stores to manage subscriptions; and authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety. If BiteWise is involved in a merger or acquisition, your information may transfer subject to this Policy. 4. Health Platform Data Data received from Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect is used only to provide fitness and nutrition features within the App. It is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for marketing. 5. Data Retention We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep it longer by law. 6. Security We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encryption in transit, to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. 7. Your Rights and Choices Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can edit most data in the App, revoke health-platform permissions in your device settings, turn off notifications, or delete your account in Settings. To make any other request, contact us using the details below. 8. Children The App is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it. 9. International Transfers Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. 10. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes in the App or by email and update the date above. Contact Us Questions about this document can be sent to support@bitewise.app.
